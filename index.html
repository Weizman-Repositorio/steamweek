STEAM Week 27

Five days of building, coding, designing and debating. We invite your school’s delegation to represent your institution and compete through real-world projects.

Dates: March 15–19, 2027 Venue: Instituto Dr. Jaim Weizman / San José / Costa Rica

The week at a glance

Across five days, students engage in hands-on workshops, collaborative exhibitions, live competitions, and showcase sessions.

Opening & Interactive Exhibitions

Opening ceremony, summative class project displays, and an interactive gym exhibition featuring recorded student presenters.

Grade-Level Mini-Workshops

Multi-session hands-on workshops by grade level where teams design, prototype, and refine their annual STEAM challenges.

Skill-Building Workshops

Cross-grade sessions covering programming and inclusion case studies, video editing, and data visualization.

Robot Competition

Multi-round tournament across qualifying stages, semi-finals, and finals in two distinct robotics categories.

Debate & WTalks

Guided preparation sessions leading into the STEAM Debate tournament and five-minute student WTalks inspired by a guest speaker.

Final Showcase & Closing

Final project presentations across all grades, awards, and the closing farewell ceremony.

Projects by grade

Every grade works on its own challenge during the mini-workshops and presents it on the final day.

5th grade · School Challenge

Design Thinking applied to real problems in our school environment.

6th grade · Resilient Cities

Earthquake-resistant structures and architecture, and a collaborative city built to face a climate event.

7th grade · Video Mapping

Projection mapping, our most complex challenge, inspired by Jewish heritage and the KKL.

8th grade · Pet Assistive Devices

Service learning: designing and building prosthetics, wheelchairs and accessories for animals with physical disabilities.

9th grade · Beach Cleanup

Engineering devices to collect microplastics and debris from sand, tested on a Thursday field trip. Grade 9 also leads the debate.

Field trip: Thursday

Robot competition

Visiting schools compete as independent teams representing their own institutions across two categories throughout the week.

Category 1 · Grades 5–6

Robotics Choreography

Teams program a synchronized, fully autonomous routine using at least four robots, blending robotics engineering with an original artistic narrative inspired by KKL or a UN Sustainable Development Goal through music, dance, and custom staging.

Category 2 · Grades 7–9

Mini All-Terrain Rescue Robot

Teams design and build a compact, suitcase-ready autonomous mini rescue robot engineered to navigate a multi-station obstacle course inspired by real rescue scenarios, facing progressively greater terrain challenges in each round.
